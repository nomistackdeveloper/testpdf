Frequently Asked Questions

Is OrioHub PDF Editor completely free and private?

Yes. OrioHub PDF Editor (pdf-editor.oriohub.com) runs 100% locally inside your web browser. Your PDF files and images are never uploaded to any server.

Can I crop and freely resize images to any custom size on a PDF page?

Yes! Select any placed image and click Crop to trim it freely or with preset ratios, then resize it freely to any custom width and height using the corner/edge handles on desktop or exact Width/Height controls on any device.

Does OrioHub PDF Editor work on mobile phones and tablets?

Yes. Mobile devices get crisp High-DPI PDF rendering, two-finger pinch-to-zoom, and dedicated button-controlled positioning and sizing so scrolling never shifts your layout by accident.
